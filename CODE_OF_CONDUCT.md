# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are correspondingly small.

**Be decent.** Harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination aren't welcome
here, in issues or anywhere else the project has a presence.

**Assume competence.** People filing bugs about lighting hardware usually know
their own machine better than you do. Ask what they observed before telling them
what they did wrong.

**Stay on topic.** Issues are for RGB Off. Problems with OpenRGB, PawnIO or a
vendor's own software belong with those projects.

Reports go to the repository owner through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/LuhOnCoffee/rgb-off/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message. I'll act on them — up to and including blocking accounts
from the repository.
